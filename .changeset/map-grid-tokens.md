New tokens `map-grid-<map>` and `map-grid-<map>-minor` for `ember`, `wave`, `viridis` and `gray`, light and dark: grid lines over a map image. Each holds 2:1 (minor 1.3:1) or more against its map's lowest and highest stops, and under 2.6:1 (minor 1.7:1) against the nearer one.
