"@eleoptics/plots": minor
---

New: `interact(el, opts)` makes a rendered 2D plot interactive, for `curve`, `rayFan`, `spot`, `throughFocus`, `bars`, typed `map2D` and `layout2D`. Hovering emits `onhover(HoverEvent)`: the nearest data point with its series, panel and values, or, between points on a drawn line, the linearly interpolated reading flagged `interpolated` with its `segment`; a map reports the sample's indices and, given `values`, its value; a `layout2D` lens element reports its glass and properties. While a point is hovered, `interact` draws a dot on it, dashed drop lines to both axes and a tooltip near the pointer. Clicking a series, bar, surface or field fan emits `onselect(SelectEvent)`. The wheel zooms about the pointer, a drag pans and a double-click or `reset()` resets, reported by `onview(view)`; the caller redraws with `view`. The host takes focus: ←/→ step points, ↑/↓ series, Enter selects, Escape clears, + and − zoom, 0 resets. New: `readout(event, { x, y })` is the tooltip's text, `<label> · <x> <unit>, <y> <unit>`. New: `view` on `curve`, `rayFan`, `spot`, `throughFocus`, typed `map2D` and `layout2D` draws that window (`{ x?, y? }` on a curve, `{ center, half }` on a spot, `{ x, y }` on a layout, which fits it at one scale; a map redraws its frame over the same bitmap); a zoomed `curve` or `layout2D` draws only its window plus 32 px, with `data-from` giving a cut run's data index. Marks carry `data-*` identity attributes (`data-series`, `data-role`, `data-label`, `data-panel`, `data-category`, `data-cell`, `data-field`, `data-ray`, `data-surface`) that `interact` reads; a layout's glass polygons carry their element's properties. Without `interact` or `view`, the drawing is unchanged apart from these attributes.
